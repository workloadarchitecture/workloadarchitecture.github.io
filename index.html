PitcherWorkload

Pitcher Workload is the coaching staff's tool for planning each pitcher's throwing program and keeping throwing workload in a safe range. Coaches build day-by-day programs; pitchers see their own plan on their phone and report what they actually threw and how they feel.

What it does

  • Daily plan for each pitcher: day type, plyos, catch play distances, mound work and games, with counts, intensities and velocity targets.
  • Workload tracking: each day's throws are turned into workload units, and the acute:chronic workload ratio (ACWR) flags days that ramp up or drop off too fast.
  • Player reports: pitchers log their throws, how their arm and body feel, and sleep, so coaches can adjust.
  • Coach tools: a calendar builder, saved plan versions, charts and printable PDF plans.

Who can use it

Only pitchers and coaches the staff has added. Sign in with the Google account your coach has on file; you see only what your role allows.
