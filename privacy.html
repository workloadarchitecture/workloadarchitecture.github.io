Pitcher Workload: privacy

Pitcher Workload is a private tool a coaching staff uses to plan pitchers' throwing programs and track throwing workload. It is only for people the staff has added.

What signing in shares

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name and email address from Google. It uses your email only to check that you are on the staff's list and to decide what you can see. It does not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

What the app stores

  • Throwing programs your coaches build for you (day types, throws, counts, intensities, notes).
  • What you report: throws, how you feel (elbow, shoulder, upper and lower body, sleep quality) and any note you add.
  • Throw data from devices such as radar guns, when the staff imports it.

This information is kept in Google Sheets owned by the staff's Google account. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything other than managing your throwing workload.

Who can see it

  • Players see only their own program and reports.
  • Coaches see the pitchers assigned to them; the staff administrator sees everyone.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To have your information corrected or removed, ask your coach or the staff administrator.
